Support

Questions, problems or a wrong answer in the app? Please open an issue at github.com/lasha3901/the-oath/issues.

Common questions

Which test do I take? If you filed Form N-400 before October 20, 2025, the 2008 test; on or after that date, the 2025 test. The app recommends one from your filing date.

Are these the official questions? Yes. The questions and accepted answers are copied word for word from USCIS documents. Translations, explanations and English practice sentences are written by The Oath and are not official.

Why does an answer say "check uscis.gov"? Some answers change with elections and appointments. Always confirm them at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates before your interview.

How do I restore my purchase? Settings › Full access › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

The Oath is an independent study app, not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency.
