The Oath

Study for the US naturalization civics test (2008 and 2025 versions) and the English reading and writing test, on iPhone and iPad. English is the language of the official questions; Georgian translations help you understand them.

  • Official USCIS questions and answers, word for word
  • Flashcards, review and practice interviews with the official rules
  • Questions read aloud; optional recording of your answers on your device
  • Works offline. No account. No tracking.

The Oath is an independent study app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by USCIS or any government agency. Always check current answers at uscis.gov/citizenship/testupdates.
