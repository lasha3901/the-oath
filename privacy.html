Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

The Oath does not collect, sell or share personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your study progress, bookmarks, interview history and test settings (test version, testing category, state, ZIP code, congressional district and any representative name you type).
  • Voice recordings of your answers, made only when you tap Record. They are never uploaded, synced or included in device backups, and you can delete them at any time in Settings.
  • Your ZIP code is used only on your device to suggest congressional districts from bundled public Census data. It is never sent anywhere.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your progress, bookmarks, interview history and test settings are stored in your own private iCloud database, which only you can access. We cannot see it. Recordings are never synced.

Answer updates

Some civics answers change after elections and appointments. The app downloads a public file of current answers from this website at most once a day. The request contains no personal information or identifiers beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address, which this site's host, GitHub, may log under its own policy).

Purchases

The one-time purchase is handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

The app is a study tool for adults preparing for naturalization and is not directed at children.

Contact

See the Support page.
